proc.widgets.description.requisitionStatus=Displays the requisitions quantity and their current status.
proc.widgets.description.requisitions=Displays the quantity of all Requisitions items per month. For every product type (Catalog, SmartForm and Punchout), there is a certain barchart.
proc.widgets.description.spendPerCatalogs=Displays a chart with top 5 catalogs (by spend), by default. It is possible to select the quantity of catalogs to be shown in the widget.
proc.widgets.description.spendPerCategory=Displays a chart with top 5 categories (by spend), by default. It is possible to select the quantity of categories to be shown in the widget.
proc.widgets.description.spendPerSupplier=Displays a chart with top 5 suppliers (by spend), by default. It is possible to select suppliers to be shown in the widget.
proc.widgets.description.spendVsVolume=Displays a chart with the orders quantity and the sum of their prices per supplier. The barchart shows the orders quantity (left axis), the line shows the total price (right axis).
proc.widgets.description.totalCartsInApproval=Displays the total quantity of the carts in approval in the current year (by default) and previous year (by selecting the corresponding check box).
proc.widgets.description.totalSpend=Displays the sum of all spends in the current year (by default) and previous year (by selecting the corresponding check box).
proc.widgets.description.totalSpendYearToDate=Displays the chart with the total spend per month for the selected period. By default, the period is from January, 1 to the current date.
proc.widgets.includeCustomerHierarchy.label=Include customer hierarchy
